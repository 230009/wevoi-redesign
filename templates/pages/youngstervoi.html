Ages 9–11 · 1–2 minutes

Speak clearly. Sound like yourself.

What should your speech cover?

Build a clear message.

  • Introduce the topic.
  • Clearly state your opinion or main idea.
  • Explain why you think this way.
  • Give relevant examples or experiences.
  • Explain the impact or importance of your idea.
  • Finish with a clear takeaway.
Current Spark example

How Kindness Can Change a Community

  • What does kindness mean?
  • Why is it important?
  • How can one act affect another person?
  • How can kindness spread through a school or community?
  • What can young people do?
