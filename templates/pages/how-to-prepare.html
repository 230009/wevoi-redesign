How to Prepare

Prepare the idea. Practise the delivery. Own the stage.

A strong speech is built before the camera turns on.

🧠
Preparation tips

Think → Structure → Practise → Present

🎯

Know Your Topic

Understand the topic and decide on the one message your audience should remember.

🔎

Use Reliable Sources

Support ideas with accurate facts, stories, examples and trustworthy information.

🧱

Structure Your Speech

Create a clear beginning, middle and end, with strong transitions between ideas.

⏱️

Practise

Rehearse aloud, focus on your ideas rather than memorising every word, and stay within time.

Delivery checklist

Make your message feel alive.

👀 Eye contact

Connect with your audience.

👐 Body language

Use natural gestures.

🔊 Voice & pace

Vary tone and pause with purpose.

✨ Be yourself

Let your personality come through.

🌬️ Stage fright

Use nervous energy positively.

📈 Keep improving

Reflect after every performance.
