Speak clearly. Sound like yourself.

What should your speech cover?

Build a clear message.

  • Introduce the issue and establish why it matters.
  • Explain your interpretation of the topic.
  • Present a strong central argument.
  • Develop multiple supporting points.
  • Use relevant examples, evidence or resources.
  • Demonstrate critical thinking and analysis.
  • Consider an opposing viewpoint.
  • Provide a meaningful rebuttal.
  • Finish with a memorable conclusion, reflection or call to action.
