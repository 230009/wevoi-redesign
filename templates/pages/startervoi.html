Ages 6–8 · 30 seconds – 1 minute

Speak clearly. Sound like yourself.

What should your speech cover?

Build a clear message.

  • Introduce what you are talking about.
  • Explain your idea or experience.
  • Share how it made you feel or what you think.
  • Give a simple example where appropriate.
  • Finish with a clear thought, lesson or message.
Current Spark example

A Time Someone Was Kind to Me

  • What happened?
  • Who was kind to you?
  • How did it make you feel?
  • Why was the kindness important?
  • What did you learn?
