Video Recording Guidelines

Clear beats complicated.

You do not need professional equipment. A smartphone, tablet, laptop or basic camera is enough when your speech can be seen and heard clearly.

📱
Simple setup

Stable · Well lit · Easy to hear

📷

Camera

Keep it stable, around eye level, with the speaker’s head and upper body clearly in frame.

☀️

Lighting

Make sure the face is clearly visible. Natural light works well; avoid a bright window behind the speaker.

🔊

Audio

Record somewhere quiet and check that every word can be heard comfortably.

🧹

Background

Choose a clean, appropriate background without unnecessary distractions.

Framing example
🎤

Eye level

Editing

Optional, not compulsory.

A simple unedited recording can achieve the same maximum score as an edited video.

CC

Captions

Subtitles can support accessibility and clarity.

🖼️

Supporting Visuals

Relevant images or keywords may support the message.

✨

Keep Effects Simple

Editing should support the speech, not replace communication skills.
