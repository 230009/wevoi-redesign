Evaluation Criteria

Four areas. One balanced performance.

Judges look at what you say, how accurately you communicate, how you deliver it and how well your speech is organised.

25%
Balanced rubric

Content · Accuracy · Delivery · Organization

25%

Content

Ideas, examples, reasoning, relevance, creativity and appropriate supporting resources.

25%

Accuracy

Intelligibility, pronunciation, grammar, vocabulary and age-appropriate language.

25%

Delivery

Confidence, voice control, fluency, body language, expression and audience engagement.

25%

Organization

Introduction, development, logical connection, structure, conclusion and clear message.

For younger speakers

Judges focus on clear ideas, personal connection, understandable language, confidence and a simple beginning-middle-ending structure.

For older speakers

Expectations grow towards evidence, critical thinking, argument development, alternative perspectives, rebuttal and purposeful audience engagement.
