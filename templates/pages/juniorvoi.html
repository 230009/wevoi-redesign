Speak clearly. Sound like yourself.

What should your speech cover?

Build a clear message.

  • Introduce the topic and explain why it matters.
  • Present a clear opinion or position.
  • Develop your main arguments.
  • Use relevant examples, experiences or information.
  • Explain the reasoning behind your views.
  • Consider another perspective where appropriate.
  • Finish with a strong conclusion or takeaway.
Current Spark example

Why Empathy Is More Important Than Ever

  • What is empathy?
  • Why does it matter?
  • What happens when people lack empathy?
  • How does empathy affect schools, communities or online spaces?
  • What could people do differently?
