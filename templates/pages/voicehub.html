How it works

Three simple actions inside VoiceHub.

1

Create your profile

Create a free WeVoi account and set up a student profile.

2

Upload your video

Add a title, topic and short description, then upload a free-topic speaking video.

3

Receive engagement

Receive likes and comments, respond to the community and keep building speaking confidence.

Community feed
